DRACO MALFOID - Mya Mascot Bio
===================================

Who is Draco Malfoid?
Draco Malfoid is the primary mascot for Mya, a retro 8-bit desktop app that
channels coding agents through an RPG-style interface. Despite the sinister name
and sharp appearance, Draco is your helpful companion through the coding journey.

Personality:
- Witty and slightly mischievous, with a dry sense of humor
- Deeply invested in your coding success (though may make sarcastic comments)
- Patient mentor figure who guides you through agent conversations
- Animated and expressive, responding to different phases of work

Appearance:
- 64x64 pixel spritesheet with five distinct states:
  * IDLE: Draco at rest, ready to help
  * THINKING: Contemplative, processing your request
  * TALKING: Engaging, delivering responses to you
  * WORKING: Active and focused, executing tasks
  * ASKING: Attentive, awaiting your approval or input

Role in Mya:
Draco serves as the visual anchor for the agent chat interface, appearing in
dialogue boxes as conversations unfold. Their animations sync with the SDK stream
events, bringing life to the retro pixel aesthetic. They're also featured in
popup notifications when tasks complete, fail, or need approval.

Design Philosophy:
Draco embodies the retro 8-bit aesthetic of Mya while maintaining a modern,
helpful personality. The name is a playful reference to literary villains—a
reminder that even seemingly antagonistic figures can be excellent allies.
