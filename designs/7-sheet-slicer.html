EDIT DEFAULT MASCOT

This is the built-in mascot. Your name and loops are kept in the app's data folder and the built-in files never change. RESET on its card brings back the built-in version.
FORMAT
One spritesheet per state, as a strip or a grid. PNG under 3 MB. A strip of 64x64 frames is used as it is. Any other sheet opens the slicer: set columns and rows, then pick the frames for the loop. Frames must be square and are scaled to 64x64. Up to 16 frames per loop. Idle is required; a state with no sheet plays the idle loop.
