12x12 glyphs with 2 px strokes, drawn with crisp edges. No anti-aliasing.

SPEC

SIZE36 px tall plus a 4 px --line border, full window width. Fill --panel when focused, --bg when the window is not focused, with all text and icons in --muted.
LEFTCloud buddy icon (10x7 pixels at 2x) and MYA, then the current screen and chat. The chat name cuts off with an ellipsis first.
STATUSRUNNING and WAITING appear only when their count is above 0. Clicking either opens INBOX filtered to that status. A new approval fills WAITING amber until you look at it.
CONTROLSThree real buttons, 46x36, labelled Minimize, Maximize or Restore, and Close. Hover: --line fill, red fill for Close. Pressed: the glyph drops 2 px. Keyboard focus: a 2 px accent outline.
DRAGGINGThe whole bar drags the window; the empty middle is the main handle. Buttons and status pills opt out. Double-clicking the bar maximizes or restores.
NARROWBelow 1100 px wide, the breadcrumb shows only the chat name and the pills show only their counts.
Windows and Linux
  new BrowserWindow({ frame: false, ... })
  bar:     -webkit-app-region: drag
  buttons: -webkit-app-region: no-drag
  buttons -> IPC -> win.minimize(),
    win.isMaximized() ? win.unmaximize() : win.maximize(),
    win.close()
  main -> renderer on 'maximize', 'unmaximize',
    'focus', 'blur' so the bar redraws
